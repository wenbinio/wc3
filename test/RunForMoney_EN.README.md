# test/RunForMoney_EN.w3x

English translation of **全員逃走中 1.5 (Run for Money)** — a Taiwanese
2010 WC3 adaptation of Fuji TV's *Run for Money: Tōsōchū*. 1–10 players
spawn as random civilians (Able-Bodied Man / Frail Woman / Spry Old Man
/ Old Granny, each with one signature escape ability) and must survive
20 minutes against invulnerable Hunters. Civilians die in one hit —
your appearance fee resets to zero and you become a spectator. The
sharp design bit: casting ANY escape ability pings every Hunter onto
you, so your abilities are a last resort, not a rotation. Four timed
missions (smash a lever, deliver a bag of misfortune, fetch a prison
key, catch a wandering child) release an extra Hunter on failure, and
one more releases unconditionally at 15:00. Survive to 20:00 and take
the 1,000,000 prize. All 105 wts strings + 3 script literals (30 sites)
translated, plus a w3i unit-group name the interrupted pass had missed;
zero chat commands exist. Gameplay byte-identical: 17 of 20 members
byte-identical incl. wtg/wct; validate-map output differs by exactly
one line (the header name); pjass findings byte-identical under a full
common.j run. The original author left their name as 未知 ("Unknown"),
so the author field reads "Unknown, translated by Serendipity".

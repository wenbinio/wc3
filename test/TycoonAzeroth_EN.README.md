# test/TycoonAzeroth_EN.w3x

English translation of **大富豪·艾泽拉斯之旅 (Tycoon: Journey Through
Azeroth)** by **World** (QQ group 453244076) — a compact Monopoly-like
for 4–6 players on an Azeroth board: roll the Dice ability to move,
claim neutral Inns from the Bank of Booty Bay and upgrade them through
three rent tiers (300/600/900), pay rent when you stop on someone
else's, sell back at half price to dodge bankruptcy, and survive
Fate/Chance tiles (archaeology digs, zeppelin mishaps, Darkmoon
Circus) plus a corner teleporter. Six WoW heroes as tokens
(Kael'thas, Sylvanas, Maiev, Illidan, Vol'jin, Tyrande); last solvent
player wins. 101 wts strings + 96 script-literal occurrences
translated, WoW proper nouns rendered in Blizzard's official English
forms; the two chat commands are exact-match ASCII with no substring
parsing anywhere, left byte-identical. Gameplay byte-identical: only
war3map.j / war3map.wts / header name differ, the other 14 named
members and all 5 anonymous members SHA-256-identical; validate-map
27/28 with an identical WARN list both sides; pjass error sets
byte-identical. The protector-mangled MPQ headerSize dword was
repaired to a true v1 value to permit the splice (game-neutral).
Author fields credit "World, translated by Serendipity".
